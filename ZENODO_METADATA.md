## Description
Reproducibility package containing the analysis scripts, derived result tables,
and principal figures for the DT-RLR study on selective adaptive reference
calibration for open-set electrical fault diagnosis under temporal drift.
The raw electrical benchmark is publicly available separately at
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20565892 and is not redistributed here.

## Keywords
open-set recognition; electrical fault diagnosis; temporal drift; concept drift;
adaptive calibration; robust local reference; conformal prediction; Page-Hinkley
